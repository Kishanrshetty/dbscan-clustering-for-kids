Meet the critters. They love to sing, but only when they feel brave — and they only feel brave in a crowd. When one sings, the song jumps from brave critter to brave critter until the whole crowd is singing. Critters far from every crowd stay quiet. That’s exactly how DBSCAN finds groups in data, without ever being told how many groups to look for.
